48 tools for running social accounts, grouped by the working week rather than by the software
market: queuing the post, writing it, cutting the clip, making the image, hearing what comes
back, reporting on it, finding the creator, and answering the messages.

No entry here is described by growth, reach, or engagement. Those numbers are decided by the
platform's ranking rather than by any product on this list, and every figure a vendor
publishes comes from its own customers in its own conditions. What each description says
instead is what the tool does differently from the ones beside it.

Maintained by [TiorAI](https://tiorai.com/), which catalogues AI tools for a living.

## Scheduling and publishing

The queue everything else eventually feeds into.

- **[Buffer](https://buffer.com/)** — The simplest of these to hand to somebody who has never scheduled a post. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com/)** — Built for approval chains, which is why regulated industries end up on it. `Paid` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Later](https://later.com/)** — Plans the grid as a picture, so a feed is designed rather than accumulated. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Loomly](https://www.loomly.com/)** — Prompts the calendar with occasions and trends before the week is empty. `Paid` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Metricool](https://metricool.com/)** — Reports on competitors from the same screen that queues your own posts. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Publer](https://publer.com/)** — Handles many accounts at once, which is the agency shape of this problem. `Freemium` `Web`
- **[Sendible](https://www.sendible.com/)** — Client dashboards and white labelling, aimed squarely at agencies. `Paid` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/)** — Recycles evergreen posts on a category schedule instead of a fixed date. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/)** — The enterprise choice, where publishing, listening, and the inbox are one contract. `Paid` `Web` `iOS` `Android`

## Writing posts and hooks

The first line, which decides whether the rest is read.

- **[FeedHive](https://www.feedhive.com/)** — Holds a post back until a condition you set is actually met. `Freemium` `Web`
- **[Hypefury](https://hypefury.com/)** — Turns a post that worked into a recurring slot in the queue. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Postwise](https://postwise.ai/)** — Writes in the voice of accounts you point it at rather than a house style. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Taplio](https://taplio.com/)** — Built only for LinkedIn, including the commenting that carries reach there. `Paid` `Web` `Browser Extension`
- **[Typefully](https://typefully.com/)** — A writing surface first, with the scheduling added around it. `Freemium` `Web`

## Short-form video and clipping

Cutting a long recording into the twenty seconds worth posting.

- **[Captions](https://www.captions.ai/)** — Shoots and edits on the phone, which is where most of this footage starts. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Klap](https://klap.app/)** — Takes a video link and returns clips without an editing timeline. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Munch](https://www.munchstudio.com/)** — Picks the moments using engagement patterns rather than transcript keywords. `Paid` `Web`
- **[OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/)** — Reframes a wide shot to vertical and keeps the speaker centred. `Freemium` `Web`
- **[Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/)** — Focused on the animated captions and effects that short-form video expects. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Vizard](https://vizard.ai/)** — Aimed at webinars and long interviews rather than at podcasts. `Freemium` `Web`

## Visual content and templates

Making the image without opening a design application.

- **[AdCreative.ai](https://www.adcreative.ai/)** — Generates many variants of one creative so a test has something to compare. `Paid` `Web`
- **[The Brief AI](https://www.thebrief.ai/)** — Resizes one design across every placement and keeps the layout sensible. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Canva](https://www.canva.com/)** — Holds the brand kit, so a stand-in can post without breaking the look. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Ocoya](https://www.ocoya.com/)** — Produces the caption and the image from one brief instead of two tools. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Predis.ai](https://www.predis.ai/)** — Turns a product page into a carousel or a short video without a brief. `Freemium` `Web`

## Listening and monitoring

What is being said when nobody tagged you.

- **[Brand24](https://brand24.com/)** — Priced within reach of a single team rather than a communications department. `Paid` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/)** — Deep historical archives, which matter when you need to show a trend. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/)** — Covers news media alongside social, which suits a communications team. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Mention](https://mention.com/)** — Alerts on a keyword the moment it appears rather than in a daily digest. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Sprinklr](https://www.sprinklr.com/)** — Joins listening to customer service, which is one system in a large company. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Talkwalker](https://www.talkwalker.com/)** — Recognises a logo in an image, so unlabelled mentions are still found. `Paid` `Web` `API`

## Analytics and reporting

Explaining last month to somebody who was not watching.

- **[Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com/)** — Scores creative against how similar images performed for the same audience. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Emplifi](https://emplifi.io/)** — Combines social analytics with commerce data for retail brands. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Iconosquare](https://www.iconosquare.com/)** — Started on Instagram and still reports it in more detail than the rest. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Keyhole](https://keyhole.co/)** — Tracks a hashtag or a campaign as its own object rather than an account. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/)** — Benchmarks against a named competitor set you choose and keep. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/)** — Exports cleanly, which matters when the report has to live in a deck. `Paid` `Web` `API`

## Creator and influencer discovery

Finding the person, and checking the audience is real.

- **[Aspire](https://www.aspire.io/)** — Runs the whole relationship, including briefs, contracts, and payment. `Paid` `Web`
- **[CreatorIQ](https://www.creatoriq.com/)** — Aimed at brands running hundreds of creators across several markets. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Grin](https://grin.co/)** — Built for ecommerce, with product seeding and sales attribution attached. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Heepsy](https://www.heepsy.com/)** — The cheapest useful search here, which suits a first campaign. `Freemium` `Web`
- **[Modash](https://www.modash.io/)** — Reports where a creator's audience actually lives before you brief them. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Upfluence](https://www.upfluence.com/)** — Searches your own customers for people who already have an audience. `Paid` `Web`

## Community and inbox management

Everything that arrives after the post goes out.

- **[Agorapulse](https://www.agorapulse.com/)** — Treats the social inbox as a queue with assignment and a closed state. `Paid` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Chatfuel](https://chatfuel.com/)** — Focused on the messaging apps rather than on the public comment thread. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Khoros](https://khoros.ai/)** — Runs an owned community forum alongside the social channels. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[ManyChat](https://manychat.com/)** — Replies to a comment with a direct message, which is the whole trick. `Freemium` `Web`
- **[respond.io](https://respond.io/)** — Merges messages from several channels into one conversation per person. `Paid` `Web` `API`

## How tools are selected

A tool qualifies when **running social accounts is the job it is sold for**: queuing the
post, writing it, cutting the clip, making the image, hearing what is said back, reporting on
it, finding the creator, or answering the messages. The buyer is a social media manager, a
community manager, or an agency running accounts for other people.

### No follower promises, and no engagement numbers

This market sells outcomes it cannot control, and that is the single thing this list refuses to
repeat.

**No entry here is described by growth, reach, or engagement.** Every vendor publishes a
multiplier, every multiplier comes from the vendor's own customers, and nobody outside can
reproduce one. More importantly, none of these products controls the outcome: the platform's
ranking does, and it changes without notice. A tool that genuinely saves an hour a day is worth
buying; a tool that promises three times the reach is promising something that is not its to
give.

**And a product whose only AI feature is a caption generator was left out**, however well it
sells. Writing a caption is the part of this job that takes the least time. The parts that take
the most are cutting video, reading the inbox, checking whether an audience is real, and
explaining the month to somebody who was not watching. That is where the categories here are
weighted.

One consequence worth stating: **the schedulers everybody already knows are still listed**, and
they appear in two other TiorAI lists as well. That is not duplication for its own sake. A
marketing list carries them as one channel among many, a small-business list carries the free
tier for an owner posting between other jobs, and this one is written for somebody whose whole
job is the feed. Same products, three different questions, three descriptions written separately.

### What the categories are telling you

The eight groups follow the working week of somebody running accounts, and they are weighted
towards the parts that take time rather than the parts that are easy to sell.

- **Scheduling and publishing** is the largest group because it is the one everybody buys first
  and the one where the products genuinely differ: an agency queue, an approval chain, and a
  one-person feed are three different problems with three different answers.
- **Listening** and **Analytics** are separate on purpose. Listening is about what other people
  said; analytics is about what your own posts did. Several vendors sell both and are bought for
  one.
- **Creator discovery** sits here rather than in the marketing list because checking whether an
  audience is real is a social media manager's job, not a media buyer's.

### What is excluded

Follower, like, and view sellers, and anything that buys engagement. Automation that follows,
unfollows, or mass-comments on the poster's behalf, which breaks every platform's terms and
puts the account at risk. Products whose only AI feature is a caption generator.

Also out: paid social advertising and media buying, which belong in `awesome-ai-marketing-tools`;
general video editors that are not built for vertical short form; link-in-bio pages with no other
function; and anything in closed beta or on a waitlist.

Entries are ordered alphabetically inside each category. Position carries no meaning, and
there is no paid placement, sponsorship, or affiliate link anywhere in this repository.

## Disclaimer

Everything listed is built and operated by a third party, not by TiorAI. Pricing, plan
limits, and terms change without notice, and a tool that fits today may not next quarter.

**Platform terms decide what is allowed, and they change more often than this list does.** Every
product here connects to accounts you do not own on infrastructure you do not control. An
integration that works today can be restricted tomorrow by an API change, and an automation
that looks harmless can still breach a platform's terms. Read those terms before you connect
anything, because the account that gets suspended is yours.

**Nothing here is scored on growth, reach, or engagement**, and the absence is deliberate. Those
numbers are decided by ranking systems the vendors do not control and cannot predict. Any
figure you are shown comes from the vendor's own customers in the vendor's own conditions.

**Listening tools collect what people said in public about other people.** That is lawful in most
places and still carries data-protection obligations in many of them, particularly once you store
it, profile it, or join it to a customer record.

Listing here is editorial selection, not endorsement.
